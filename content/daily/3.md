One European studio expansion today, plus two breakdowns showing how far environment work is being pushed on post-apocalyptic shows.

## Studio expansion into Europe

Outpost now offers a German base with access to regional and federal incentives, plus a senior creature team. Expect it to compete for the same European episodic work, and possibly undercut on incentive-led pricing.

- [Outpost VFX Opens in Germany, Taps 'House of the Dragon' Vet Sven Martin](https://www.awn.com/news/outpost-vfx-opens-germany-taps-house-dragon-vet-sven-martin) · awn.com

## Environment work on post-apocalyptic shows

Both breakdowns lean on large 3D city builds over matte painting, with BlueBolt using hundreds of assets and Framestore painting only the focal points. Useful reference when scoping wide-shot environments: asset counts and camera freedom drive cost, and selective detail keeps it down.
