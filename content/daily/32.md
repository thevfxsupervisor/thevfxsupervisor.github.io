France is paying more for VFX just as UK inward spend falls, and the US federal credit faces political risk. For bids, location and rebate maths matter more this quarter. AI is also arriving inside the editing and review tools clients already use, so expect questions about it.

## Update: France's rebate gets bigger

Update: France has widened its rebate so non-EU actors' salaries now count as eligible spend, and CNC reports a jump in scouting requests from Hollywood. VFX spend there rose in 2025. Worth pricing France against Italy and Spain on any bid with a large VFX share.

- [How France overhauled investment infrastructure and tax rebate in bid to woo large scale film and TV projects](https://www.screendaily.com/features/how-france-overhauled-investment-infrastructure-and-tax-rebate-in-bid-to-woo-large-scale-film-and-tv-projects/5222225.article) · screendaily.com *The 40% rate for local VFX spend over €2m and rising US interest make French vendors or a French split worth pricing*

## Update: UK inward spend slumps

Update: UK film and TV spend fell sharply in the first three quarters of 2026, with inward investment hit hardest. Co-production is the exception. Expect tougher competition for UK-based work, and consider co-production structures when pitching.

- [Inward film spend in UK down 40% from January-September 2026, but co-production up 31%](https://www.screendaily.com/news/inward-film-spend-in-uk-down-40-from-january-september-2026-but-co-production-up-31/5222256.article) · screendaily.com

## US federal credit: promising but fragile

A federal production credit could pull work back to the US, and stacked with state credits it may reach up to 57%. That would change where US-funded shows choose to shoot and post. The bill's fate depends on politics, so treat it as upside, not a planning assumption.

- [Democrats Won. When Do Hollywood Crews Get Back to Work?](https://www.indiewire.com/features/interviews/hollywood-jobs-after-midterms-film-tax-credits-1235226539/) · indiewire.com *The lame-duck push is the first test, and a political fallout could stall a credit that would shift US-funded work*
- [“We need to bring Tom Cruise home,” say cheerleaders of US federal incentive](https://www.screendaily.com/news/we-need-to-bring-tom-cruise-home-say-cheerleaders-of-us-federal-incentive/5222266.article) · screendaily.com *Up to 57% combined with state credits would be a strong lure for US productions, so worth watching if it advances*

## AI moves into the tools you already use

AI is arriving inside editing, review and studio workflows rather than as separate apps. Clients may arrive with AI-made placeholders or edits, which affects how you scope and price shots. A studio tying up with a tool maker also hints at where in-house pipelines may head.

- [Adobe Brings AI Agents to After Effects, Premiere at Adobe MAX 2026](https://www.awn.com/news/adobe-brings-ai-agents-after-effects-premiere-adobe-max-2026) · Animation World Network *Agent assistants in After Effects and Premiere could change how quickly clients expect comps and revisions, so check your own pipeline*
- [Avid’s Ready to Play Brings Google AI Models to Media Composer](https://postperspective.com/avids-ready-to-play-brings-google-ai-models-to-media-composer/) · postperspective.com *Editors can now generate placeholder shots and remove objects like boom mics, which may reshape what lands in your VFX turnover*
- [The Last Mile: How Rodeo FX Brought xSTUDIO Into Daily Production](https://www.aswf.io/blog/how-rodeo-fx-brought-xstudio-to-production/) · aswf.io *A two-engineer team adapting an open source review tool shows a small studio can build custom pipeline tools cheaply*
- [Adobe and Disney Announce Multi-Year AI Alliance](https://www.awn.com/news/adobe-and-disney-announce-multi-year-ai-alliance) · Animation World Network *Disney's custom models trained on its own properties suggest some studio work may go to in-house AI, so worth watching*
- [iQIYI Launches Chinas First AI Film Studio in Shanghai, with Inaugural Title Cop in Trouble in Production](https://en.antaranews.com/news/441719/iqiyi-launches-chinas-first-ai-film-studio-in-shanghai-with-inaugural-title-cop-in-trouble-in-production) · en.antaranews.com *A Chinese platform pairing live action with AI post is a possible sign of cheaper rivals, though this is a press release*

## Credited work and skills funding

Credited shows are useful pricing and positioning references, and public skills money is open to organisations that train people in AI and virtual production. If you plan to go independent, these are routes to credibility and new skills, not direct work.

- [Charting the Visual Effects for 'One Piece' Season 2](https://www.awn.com/vfxworld/charting-visual-effects-one-piece-season-2) · awn.com *A 4,058-shot season run with returning vendors shows long-running shows reward vendors who stay close to the supervisor and producer*
- [Ferine: EDI Effetti Digitali Italiani Brings a Panther to Life](https://www.artofvfx.com/ferine-edi-effetti-digitali-italiani-brings-a-panther-to-life/) · artofvfx.com *A creature-focused making-of from an Italian studio shows what a smaller shop can show off to win creature work*
- [NFTS, Falmouth University awarded £650,000 from BFI to support industry use of AI, virtual production](https://www.screendaily.com/news/nfts-falmouth-university-awarded-650000-from-bfi-to-support-industry-use-of-ai-virtual-production/5222296.article) · screendaily.com *Funded AI and virtual production R&D for UK independents may produce free findings you can use when advising clients*
- [Screen Ireland and Northern Ireland Screen open €1.25m Shared Horizons Fund for all-island skills](https://www.scannain.com/irish/shared-horizons-fund-screen-ireland-northern-ireland-screen/) · scannain.com *The 1 December deadline and two-partner rule favour organisations, so you would likely need to join a consortium to benefit*
