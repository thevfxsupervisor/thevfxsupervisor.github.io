Framestore is selling previs, virtual production and AI-led development as one front-end, while Disguise changes hands and Unreal adds agents. For an independent, the cost question is shifting to who owns the early decisions. Price AI work by retakes, not renders.

## Framestore bundles pre-production

Update: Framestore has created a Global Head of Pre-production Services role, covering art department, visual development, visualisation and virtual production across four studios. Its visual development team will also incubate its AI work. This suggests big houses will pitch the early stages and final VFX as one package, which independents may need to answer.

- [Framestore unifies pre production under Sarah Cauchois](https://www.televisual.com/news/framestore-unifies-pre-production-under-sarah-cauchois/) · televisual.com *Framestore now offers early design through to final VFX under one lead, so small teams competing for early-stage work face a bundled rival*

## Virtual production ownership and tooling

The software behind LED volumes and the engine that drives them are both in motion. A new owner at Disguise is unlikely to change your kit overnight, but it is worth watching. Unreal's agent features are preview only, so keep them out of any quoted pipeline for now.

- [Yamaha Disguise Acquisition: A Bold £215m Bet on Great Shows](https://www.globalbrandsmagazine.com/yamaha-disguise-acquisition/) · globalbrandsmagazine.com *Disguise tools run virtual production stages, and Yamaha expects to close in December, so watch for changes to pricing or support*
- [Unreal Engine 5.9 Preview: Agents, Canvas and a Browser-Sized Ambition](https://digitalproduction.com/2026/11/16/unreal-engine-5-9-preview-agents-canvas-and-a-browser-sized-ambition/) · digitalproduction.com *Agent tools in the editor could speed in-engine previs and scene work, but Preview status means test them, do not bid on them*

## Notable shows and who did the work

Much of this work is meant to go unseen: period clean-up, modest CG animals, subtle face tweaks. Shot counts are small and credits sit with named supervisors. That is useful reference for pitching invisible, supervisor-led work at a modest scale.

- [The Rays and Shadows (Les Rayons et les Ombres): The Mill Recreates Occupied France with Invisible VFX](https://www.artofvfx.com/the-rays-and-shadows-les-rayons-et-les-ombres-the-mill-recreates-occupied-france-with-invisible-vfx/) · artofvfx.com *A 163-shot period job built on anachronism removal is a clear model for the invisible work a small team can bid*
- [The Mill Sets CG Wild Boars Loose in 'Too Many Beasts'](https://www.awn.com/news/mill-sets-cg-wild-boars-loose-too-many-beasts) · Animation World Network *Thirty-five shots including CG animals shows a small, creature-led package can sit inside a festival-winning feature*
- [Framestore Recreates Steinbeck's California for 'East of Eden'](https://www.awn.com/news/framestore-recreates-steinbecks-california-east-eden) · Animation World Network *Framestore's New York team led a Netflix period series with one master CG environment, a useful template for environment-heavy episodic bids*
- [Behind the tools and techniques used to craft those subtle facial enhancements in ‘Obsession’](https://beforesandafters.com/2026/11/17/behind-the-tools-and-techniques-used-to-craft-those-subtle-facial-enhancements-in-obsession/) · befores & afters *Subtle 2D face work plus practical make-up, led by the production VFX supervisor, shows a light toolset can carry a headline effect*
- [Lanterns: Greg Steele (Production VFX Supervisor) & Eddie Bonin (Production VFX Producer)](https://www.artofvfx.com/lanterns-greg-steele-production-vfx-supervisor-eddie-bonin-production-vfx-producer/) · artofvfx.com *The brief to shoot it practically first points to a tighter VFX scope and more supervisor time spent on prep*

## AI in post: what is usable and what it costs

The practical use of AI is on footage you already have, not whole generated scenes. That makes it easier to quote, but retakes are the hidden cost. Check any vendor claim and shot-length limit before you put it in a bid.

- [Beeble SwitchX 3.0: new features and availability](https://www.redsharknews.com/beeble-switchx-3-0-dynamic-backgrounds-references) · redsharknews.com *Beeble says SwitchX 3.0 improves camera tracking, which could reduce matchmove work for background swaps, but longer shots may still need splitting into sections, so test first.*
- [HPA NET Roundtable: AI as a Second Unit](https://postperspective.com/hpa-net-roundtable-ai-as-a-second-unit/) · postperspective.com *Post teams say retakes drive AI cost, so build take counts and review time into any AI line in your quote*
