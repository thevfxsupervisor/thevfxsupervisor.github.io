Framestore's Sky spot puts AI inside a conventional VFX pipeline on a paid job, while cinematographers argue about it publicly. Meanwhile DC gets a new creative chief and buyers keep consolidating. Worth watching: how clients start asking for AI in bids, and who now signs off at DC.

## AI and volumes inside real pipelines

Framestore used AI for previs, environments and character removal, with named supervisors and human control kept in place. That is a template for how clients may expect AI to be priced and credited. The volume piece is a reminder to scope LED work honestly: wide shots can end up as full VFX.

- [Framestore Brings Hybrid AI/VFX to Sky's Christmas Campaign](https://www.awn.com/news/framestore-brings-hybrid-aivfx-skys-christmas-campaign) · awn.com *Gives you the credit structure and task split of a hybrid AI/VFX job, useful when writing your own AI line items*
- [Framestore delivers previs, hybrid AI and animation for Sky’s Christmas campaign](https://www.tvbeurope.com/production-post/framestore-delivers-previs-hybrid-ai-and-animation-for-skys-christmas-campaign) · tvbeurope.com *Shows AI used for previs, matte painting and character removal alongside traditional comp, a model for pitching controlled AI use*
- [Cinematographers’ Favorite Festival Is All About AI This Year, but Artists Still Hate the Tech](https://www.indiewire.com/news/festivals/camerimage-2026-is-all-about-ai-1235226124/) · indiewire.com *Cinematographers voiced bleak views while directors sounded more open, so expect clients on the same job to disagree about AI*
- [Standardising the illusion](https://britishcinematographer.co.uk/standardising-the-illusion/) · britishcinematographer.co.uk *A working DP says true wide shots in a volume usually become full VFX shots, which affects how you scope and bid*
- [Cloud Imperium Games expands VERSA Studios partnership with Squadron 42 showcase and BTS filming](https://britishcinematographer.co.uk/cloud-imperium-games-expands-versa-studios-partnership/) · britishcinematographer.co.uk *A new large volume in Manchester, built with a specialist partner, is another UK stage option, though this job was event work*

## DC leadership change

Gunn and Safran leave DC's top jobs and Jeremy Latcham takes over on 1 December, reporting into Skydance. A new head of film, TV and animation could reshape slate priorities and vendor relationships, which may open or close doors. Treat that as interpretation until his first decisions land.

- [DC Studios Shakeup: James Gunn, Peter Safran Stepping Down as Co-CEOs, Marvel Vet Jeremy Latcham Named Chief Creative Officer](https://www.indiewire.com/news/breaking-news/dc-studios-shakeup-james-gunn-peter-safran-out-jeremy-latcham-1235226240/) · indiewire.com *Mixed box office and a new creative chief suggest DC's slate and vendor choices could change, so watch early announcements*
- [DC Studios Co-Chiefs James Gunn, Peter Safran Step Down](https://www.awn.com/news/dc-studios-co-chiefs-james-gunn-peter-safran-step-down) · Animation World Network

## Update: incentives and where productions go

Update: Ireland is now the clearest example of runaway production chasing incentives, with record spend and stages often full. Canada has also now said how its C$600m funding package will be split. Both affect where your shows shoot and which co-production routes are open.

- [Carney gov’t sets out how Canada’s $425m funding boost will be shared](https://www.c21media.net/news/carney-govt-sets-out-how-canadas-425m-funding-boost-will-be-shared-out/) · Canada's federal government has set out how its C$600m (US$425m) relief package for audiovisual and audio industries will be shared, which could ease funding pressure for producers. *Money flows mainly through existing Canadian funders, with the CMF running new programmes, so check what reaches production and training*
