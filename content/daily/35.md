A large VFX group reports strong demand, and a London post house adds a senior VFX lead to sell VFX alongside colour, sound and finishing. Both suggest bigger vendors are tightening their grip on work. Independents may need a sharper pitch on where they fit.

## Prime Focus results

Update: The September quarter brought revenue up 35.2% and a return to profit after a June-quarter loss, which suggests demand at a large vendor is firm. The company also disclosed tax department searches in October and says it has received no demand or notice. Worth watching for any knock-on to DNEG's capacity or pricing.

## Harbor's London VFX hire

Update: Harbor has named Danny Duke, formerly Production Director and Head of Film & TV at Absolute Post, as its Head of VFX Features & Episodic. The London VFX team is now more than 40 artists. Pitches may increasingly bundle VFX with colour, sound and finishing, so independents should be ready to show how they fit alongside that.

- [Harbor Taps Danny Duke to Lead Features and Episodic VFX as London Team Expands](https://mande.net/es/btl/harbor-taps-danny-duke-to-lead-features-and-episodic-vfx-as-london-team-expands) · mande.net *A vendor offering VFX plus finishing under one roof could shape how producers weigh your bid, especially on episodic work*
