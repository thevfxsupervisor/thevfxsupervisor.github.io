State-funded virtual production space and treaty co-productions are giving small teams routes to big-studio capability. Godzilla Minus Zero's opening suggests VFX-led work from outside Hollywood can still draw a global audience. Worth watching: how the Academy treats AI-built animation.

## Public virtual production capacity

Korea is putting a large LED stage with 360-degree tracking into public hands for small producers. That could lower the cost of a VP option in a bid. It may also pull some regional work towards state-backed facilities. Worth watching for similar schemes elsewhere.

- [CJ OliveNetworks Wins South Korea's KOCCA Contracts for Daejeon Virtual Production Studio and DMS UHD Post-Production Upgrade, Targeting December Completion](https://finance.biggo.com/news/46b433ef-ccae-46b6-9ff7-c73c44df8a09) · finance.biggo.com *A public LED wall of 18.5m by 8m, aimed at small producers, could undercut private VP stage costs in Korean-linked bids*
- [When the actor moves, the virtual background follows the actor... CJ OliveNetworks wins K-content production facility project](https://www.mk.co.kr/en/culture/12202135) · mk.co.kr *The stated aim of cutting costs and timelines, plus UHD/HDR post for OTT delivery specs, shows what public facilities will pitch against independents*

## Co-production routes for animation

A treaty co-production pairing an Australian animation studio with a new Dublin post and VFX house is a template for splitting work across territories. A planned slate of seven features could mean steady work for partner studios. The job estimate is the studio's own claim.

- [Brisbane’s Like a Photon Creative looks to Ireland for animated feature Familiar](https://www.screenhub.com.au/news/news/brisbanes-like-a-photon-creative-looks-to-ireland-for-animated-feature-familiar-2710493/) · screenhub.com.au *Elephant Goldfish's role on a seven-film slate shows a new Irish VFX and post partner for animation work, with finance tied to the treaty*

## AI-built animation and the Oscars

If an AI-built feature competes with a hand-crafted stop-motion film, the Academy may have to say what counts as animation. The outcome could shape how clients view ML-heavy pipelines and how you describe your own methods in a bid.

- [VFXwire - VFX news: visual effects, film, games & animation](https://vfxwire.com/) · vfxwire.com *An awards ruling on an AI-built feature could set the tone for how clients and voters treat ML-assisted work*

## Godzilla and the value of VFX-led films

Minus Zero's opening follows a predecessor that won the VFX Oscar as a non-English-language film. That suggests a strong VFX reputation can carry a franchise sequel internationally. Creature credits from this team may be worth tracking for future hiring and bidding.
