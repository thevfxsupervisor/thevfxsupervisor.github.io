Two items today: an Australian studio shows how rebates and time zones win US post work, and Netflix details how widely it now uses AI in production.

## Incentives and time zones shape where post goes

KOJO credits a rebate of up to 45 per cent, a favourable exchange rate and overnight turnaround for lifting US work. Expect Copenhagen bids to be compared against that kind of package, especially on shows shot elsewhere and finished abroad.

- [KOJO Celebrates 30 Years as US Demand Drives Australian Post-Production Growth](https://www.filmink.com.au/public-notice/kojo-celebrates-30-years-as-us-demand-drives-australian-post-production-growth/) · filmink.com.au *US demand is growing Australian post-production work*

## AI moves into mainstream production workflows

Netflix says AI is in use across around 300 titles, including previs and VFX, and Eyeline used generative tools for crowds on one title. Clients may start asking for hybrid pricing, so work out where generative steps could cut your own crowd and previs lines.
