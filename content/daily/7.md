A start-up studio taking a full-CG environment role and an associate VFX supervisor credit on a feature is a useful signal for anyone bidding mid-size work.

## Small studios taking on feature-scale work

Roxel Creative's first major project was a full feature, with its founder also serving as associate VFX supervisor on the film. That suggests newer, leaner studios can compete for sizeable scope. Worth watching as a possible partner or rival on future bids, especially for CG environment extensions of practical sets.
