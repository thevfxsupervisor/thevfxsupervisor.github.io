## Small studios taking on feature-scale work

Roxel Creative's first major project was the feature Backrooms, where founder Calvin Romeyn also served as associate VFX supervisor on the entire movie, suggesting a strong debut for the studio. That suggests newer, leaner studios can compete for sizeable scope. Worth watching as a possible partner or rival on future bids, especially for CG environment extensions of practical sets.
