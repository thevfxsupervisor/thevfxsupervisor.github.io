Lead-vendor wins on big and mid-size shows set the benchmark you bid against, and some now openly use generative AI. Standards work on USD and a shaky freelance picture in the UK and California are the context to watch when pricing and staffing.

## Shot breakdowns and show credits

Reels show who holds lead-vendor slots and at what scale: a 418-shot lead at one end, sole-vendor 300 to 380 shot packages at the other. Useful for calibrating your own bids. The BOT VFX note on generative AI beside CG suggests AI is entering delivered work.

- [Digital Domain Breaks Down 418 VFX Shots for Spielberg's 'Disclosure Day'](https://www.awn.com/news/digital-domain-breaks-down-418-vfx-shots-spielbergs-disclosure-day) · awn.com *Sets a scale marker: a lead vendor with nearly 400 artists, plus a new creature muscle system that may raise the bar on creature bids*
- [Lugundtrug breaks down the VFX of Der Medicus II with more than 380 shots](https://foro3d.com/en/2026/octubre/lugundtrug-desglosa-los-vfx-de-der-medicus-ii-con-mas-de-380-planos.html) · foro3d.com *A German studio as sole VFX provider on 380-plus shots shows what a single European vendor can take on, relevant to Nordic and European competition*
- [Young Washington: BOT VFX Recreates 18th-Century America with Invisible Effects](https://www.artofvfx.com/young-washington-bot-vfx-recreates-18th-century-america-with-invisible-effects/) · artofvfx.com *BOT VFX credits generative AI alongside CG on 300-plus shots, a sign AI use is being openly stated in delivered work*
- [Framestore Revisits the CG Joey and 300-Strong Cavalry of 'War Horse'](https://www.awn.com/news/framestore-revisits-cg-joey-and-300-strong-cavalry-war-horse) · Animation World Network *Mostly clean-up and replication rather than hero creature work, a reminder that much of a bid is invisible fixes, and that is worth pricing*

## Update: virtual production in Europe

Update: the new piece looks at Europe-wide LED stage growth, with Nordic pop-up stage use named. It also flags readiness gaps among smaller companies, which may limit VP demand and shape what clients ask you to quote.

- [Beyond the backlot: Reinventing drama in the age of virtual production](https://nordiskfilmogtvfond.com/news/stories/beyond-the-backlot-reinventing-drama-in-the-age-of-virtual-production) · nordiskfilmogtvfond.com *Nordic VP use is real but skills and cost barriers remain, so expect uneven client demand and tight planning requirements*

## USD standards in the pipeline

An open test bed is exposing where USD handling differs between tools, including renderers lighting the same asset differently. If you run or plan a USD pipeline, this is free validation material and an early warning on interchange problems with partner vendors.

- [Academy Software Foundation’s USD Working Group Launches USD Collaborative Projects Initiative](https://www.aswf.io/blog/academy-software-foundations-usd-working-group-launches-usd-collaborative-projects-initiative/) · aswf.io *Worth pulling into your own pipeline tests before relying on USD for cross-vendor asset handover*

## Update: incentives and freelance labour

Update: California is discussing lifting or removing its cap, though lawmakers call it a heavy lift. The UK Budget disappointed the freelancer union over tax timing. Both bear on where work goes and on freelancer cashflow and rates.

- [UK Budget ‘missed opportunity’ to help freelancers claims Bectu chief Childs](https://www.c21media.net/news/uk-budget-missed-opportunity-to-help-freelancers-claims-bectu-chief-childs/) · c21media.net *Earlier tax payments could squeeze UK freelancers' cashflow, which may feed into rate demands if you hire from the UK*
