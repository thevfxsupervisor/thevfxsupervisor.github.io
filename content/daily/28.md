An all-in-one AI filmmaking platform is pitching itself at work that independent VFX teams are usually hired for. Worth deciding now how your bids treat AI, before clients ask.

## AI moves into the production pipeline

Tools are being sold as covering story, assets, VFX, editing and grade in one place, with logs of models, prompts and costs. That could shift what clients expect to pay for, and what they ask you to show. Worth having a clear stance on AI use, tracking and human review.
