Weta's chief says big clients have moved from banning generative AI to expecting vendors to have a plan for it, which bears directly on how you pitch. Stage supply and incentive rules are shifting too, and those feed straight into bid budgets.

## Update: incentives and market conditions

Update: the new items are about money in the budget. A proposed US federal credit and Hungary's lifted cap could change where shows are priced and shot. Treat both as inputs to scenario bids, not settled facts.

- [“Cautious” buyers head to AFM at critical time for independent sector](https://www.screendaily.com/features/cautious-buyers-head-to-afm-at-critical-time-for-independent-sector/5222025.article) · screendaily.com *A proposed 20% transferable US credit, stackable with state incentives, could pass by early next year, so model it in US-shoot bids*
- [Can Hungary rebuild film industry trust in the post-Orban era?](https://www.screendaily.com/features/can-hungary-rebuild-film-industry-trust-in-the-post-orban-era/5222105.article) · screendaily.com *Hungary's cap on its 30% rebate was removed by July, so Budapest is again priceable after producers lost projects*

## Shows and who did the work

Breakdowns give you scope benchmarks for mid-size vendors and show how small houses win and credit work. Use them for team size, schedule and shot-count comparisons when you scope your own bids.
