A visualisation house is adding a permanent East Coast base while the streamer trims headcount. Buyer-side cost pressure may reach vendor budgets and bids. Worth watching whether Proof's expansion pulls previs work away from European shops.

## Studio openings and buyer cuts

Proof is growing its footprint in a new market, which suggests more competition for previs and virtual production bids tied to US directors. Netflix's cuts hit product, tech, legal and HR, not production, but wider layoffs may mean tighter buyer budgets and harder rate talks.

- [Ron Frankel Brings Proof Home to Manhattan with New York Studio](https://www.awn.com/news/ron-frankel-brings-proof-home-manhattan-new-york-studio) · awn.com *A permanent Proof base in New York, with Ari Aster's next feature as first project, adds a rival for visualisation bids on US-led projects*
- [Netflix lays off 6% of workforce in streamer’s biggest cull](https://www.screendaily.com/news/netflix-lays-off-6-of-workforce-in-streamers-biggest-cull/5221473.article) · screendaily.com *Update: Netflix is cutting roughly 6% of staff, not in production teams, but it points to cost discipline that may reach vendor budgets*

## Pipeline and production work

Framestore's creature work on a returning streaming show is a reference point for how long-running episodic VFX is credited and sold.
