Disguise is heading to Yamaha, which may matter if you specify its stages, though nothing changes at closing. The US credit push has a short lame-duck window. October's starts skewed smaller, and the next big bids are likely to cluster in February to April.

## Yamaha buys Disguise

Press reports put the price at about $283 million, with closing expected in December. Nothing changes for current projects: brand, teams and customers stay as they are, and roadmap news is due in 2027. If you spec Disguise stages, watch whether future joint products stay open to rival gear.

- [Yamaha to acquire Disguise in expansion of visual technology business](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/11/13/yamaha-to-acquire-disguise-in-expansion-of-visual-technology-business/) · newscaststudio.com, also reported by redsharknews.com and techtimes.com *Revenue slipped from £67m to £60m and Unreal-based rivals are pressing at the high end, so expect pricing and support to be tested*

## US federal film credit

The sticking point is finding a legislative vehicle, and one congressman called passage in the lame-duck session 'pretty low'. Do not price a US credit into bids yet.

- [Amid Flight Halt, Trump Meets With Jon Voight to Talk U.S. Film Incentive: ‘Get This Done!’](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/trump-hasnt-commented-flight-shutdown-promoting-film-incentive-1236947426/) · variety.com *The rebate would cover domestic labour only, and the lame-duck window is short, so treat it as upside rather than a bid assumption*
- [Trump Says Federal Film Tax Credit Is Gaining Momentum](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/trump-federal-film-tax-credit-gaining-momentum-1236755258/) · hollywoodreporter.com *Trump says momentum is building, a sign the push continues, though the headline alone gives no sign of passage*

## Vendor splits and delivery at scale

Update: Ramayana's scale is now stated: 9,500 shots over a 600-day post schedule, with creature design the weak point in reviews.

- [Wētā FX Goes Out on 29 Limbs to Create 'Resident Evil' VFX](https://www.awn.com/vfxworld/w-t-fx-goes-out-29-limbs-create-resident-evil-vfx) · awn.com *A practical-first director still needed digital to take over what could not be built, a useful pitch angle for hybrid work*
- [Ramayana’s VFX Verdict : Epic Scale Meets Digital Friction](https://www.outlookindia.com/art-entertainment/ramayanas-vfx-verdict-epic-scale-meets-digital-friction) · outlookindia.com *Delivery at 9,500 shots gets judged in public, and creatures and crowds drew the sharpest criticism, so bid those with care*

## Dark Slope's Vancouver stage

Update: the Vancouver capacity now has a location and a date. A new Dark Slope stage at North Shore Studios is due to open in December. It gives productions in Vancouver an LED volume option on an eight-stage campus.

- [Dark Slope Announces New Virtual Production Stage at North Shore Studios in Vancouver](https://www.auganix.org/xr-news-dark-slope-virtual-production-stage-north-shore-studios/) · auganix.org *A December opening gives you a named Vancouver volume to quote, plus a greyboxing lab on site*

## Production volume and budgets

Only one XL start sits in the next three months, then the calendar fills from February to April, with the UK holding the deepest forward XL pipeline.

- [October Rises 9% as Budgets Skew Smaller](https://lbbonline.com/news/october-rises-9-as-budgets-skew-smaller) · lbbonline.com *Expect tight budgets on smaller shows now, and big-show bidding to pick up from February, with the UK a strong market*
