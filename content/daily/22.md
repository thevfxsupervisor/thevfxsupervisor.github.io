Capacity is being added in places where work is thin, and AI is being sold as a way to cut crew. For a Copenhagen bidder, that suggests more rivals chasing the same shows at lower prices. Worth watching: who builds virtual production and previs capacity, and where.

## New capacity, thin demand

Stages and previs hubs are opening while New Mexico spend is far below its peak. Incentive-backed regions with virtual production can undercut on price. Expect more places pitching the same shows, and check which of them can actually deliver.

- [Albuquerque's The Lot Film Campus Opens on Former GE Site](https://hoodline.com/2026/11/albuquerque-s-the-lot-aims-to-add-production-capacity-as-new-mexico-s-film-market-rebuilds/) · hoodline.com *Adds that the $50 million a year is only a target, so treat the campus as capacity, not proven work*

## AI and crew demand

One Thai executive expects crew demand to fall sharply and plans a hybrid film at half the usual budget. It is a single view, not a measured trend. Still, budgets like that could set clients' price expectations on bids.

- [AI set to slash demand for film crews](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3346909/ai-set-to-slash-demand-for-film-crews) · bangkokpost.com *A claimed 40% crew cut and a halved budget target may shape client price expectations, though the forecast is one executive's view*
