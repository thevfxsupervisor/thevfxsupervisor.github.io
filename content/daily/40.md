## US federal credit

Update: the UK government has now responded publicly, saying it takes the threat incredibly seriously. The bill text is more specific: a 20% credit on US labour, rising to 30%, for projects over US$1m with 75% of production in the US. Worth watching whether UK support follows the words.

- [UK minister says government takes threat of US federal TV tax credit ‘incredibly seriously’](https://www.c21media.net/news/uk-minister-says-government-takes-threat-of-us-federal-tv-tax-credit-incredibly-seriously/) · c21media.net *A UK minister's pledge of support shows the threat is taken seriously, but no UK measure is named, so keep UK bid assumptions cautious*

## Open-source leadership

ASWF underpins OpenEXR, OpenColorIO, MaterialX and ACES, which sit in many pipelines. A new director with studio and generative-tech experience may shape which tools get engineering support, which affects what you can standardise on without a big studio behind you.

- [Academy Software Foundation Appoints Andrew Pearce as Executive Director](https://www.aswf.io/news/andrew-pearce-new-executive-director/) · aswf.io *Pearce takes over on 1 January 2027 and says he will grow the project portfolio and engineering support, which could affect the shared tools you rely on*

## Tools and pipelines

Agentic AI is now arriving in mainstream DCCs, though still as tech preview with safeguards. USD is also being pushed into production at scale. Both could change how you scope pipeline set-up and automation, and what you tell clients about data handling.

- [Autodesk releases Maya 2027.3 with new agentic AI features](https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/11/autodesk-releases-maya-2027-3-with-new-agentic-ai-features/) · cgchannel.com *Update: Maya can now change scenes from text prompts in tech preview, with script approval and 45-day online prompt storage that clients may ask about*
