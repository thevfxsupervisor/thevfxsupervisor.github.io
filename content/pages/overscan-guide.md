### Day one :: Your first issue, in three answers
Your first issue can be in your inbox today. After our call I email you one sign-in link: open it and press `Get started`. There is no password. The link works once; if it runs out, ask me for a fresh one.

Three short questions follow, and we can answer them together on the call:

1. **What do you follow?** Say it the way you would tell a friend: who your customers are, and what they should hear about first.
2. **Where and when?** By email and on a web page, or on the web page only. How often, what time, and your time zone.
3. **Where should Overscan start looking?** Websites, feeds or keywords, one per line, or tick a starter set.

Press `Make my newsletter`. For the first issue today, open `Issues` in the top menu and press `Make an issue now`. Overscan starts on it within ten minutes and emails it to you.

Bookmark the page you land on. Signed out later? Type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives by email.

### Make it yours :: Hit reply. The next issue listens.
Teach Overscan the way you would teach a new hire: reply to any issue in your own words. Overscan answers in the same thread with what it understood, and the next issue follows it.

[[exchange]]

What a reply can change:

- **Length**: shorter, longer, or how many stories.
- **Topics**: more of one thing, less of another.
- **What matters to you**: a new sentence about your customers, or a better one in place of an old one.
- **Websites**: paste a link to add it, name one to drop it, name it again to bring it back.
- **Tone and layout**: how it sounds, how each story is laid out, an emoji on each heading.
- **Extras**: add or drop the audio, the slides or the video.

Reply `UNDO` on its own to take back the last change. Reply from the address your issue goes to: Overscan acts only on replies from you, and a forward to a colleague changes nothing.

A reply cannot move the days or the time your issue arrives. Change those on the `Brief` page in the top menu, under `When it arrives`. Every change, however you made it, is listed at the bottom of that page.

### Every format :: Read it, skim it, hear it, watch it, flip it.
Take each issue the way your morning allows. Overscan crafts it once as a letter, and turns the same issue into an audio overview, a short explainer video and slides. Pick the one that leads your email; the extras you turn on play on the issue's page in your account.

**Pick yours.** Tell me which one should lead your email and I switch it for you, any time. Turn the extras on or off yourself: tick them on the `Brief` page under `Extras`, or reply "add the slides".

**When a piece is missing.** Audio, video and slides come once they are ready and pass a check against the stories, and not every issue gets every piece. If yours is still being made, Overscan holds your copy for it, for up to a few hours. If it does not come, you get the full letter with one line saying so.

**Your customers.** Today the issue and its extras come to you, and you pass on what you like through the tool you already use. Each customer picking their own is coming, below.

### What goes in :: Turn off a website in one tap. Turn it back on in one more.
You decide where Overscan looks. Open `Sources` in the top menu: every website, feed, forum and channel on your list has a `Turn off` button. Press it and Overscan drops that one, and never adds it back by itself. `Turn back on` brings it straight back. `Delete` takes it off your list.

Or reply "drop" with the site's name, and Overscan confirms you won't see anything from it any more.

Overscan also finds new websites your stories came from. They show up marked `Added for you`, and the next issue lists them at the top under "New sources for you", each with a way to remove it. Overscan pauses the ones that turn out not to be useful. Turn one back on yourself and it stays on, marked `Kept on`, unless it stops answering or starts sending junk.

Away for a week? `Pause this newsletter` at the bottom of every issue stops it, and the same page starts it again.

### Every morning :: What lands in your inbox, and why you can trust it
At the time you picked, Overscan turns the day's news in your trade into one issue with your newsletter's name on it.

1. **Overscan catches anything new** from every website, feed, forum and channel you follow.
2. **Overscan keeps what matters to your customers.** A story you have already had stays out, unless something changed. Then it comes back marked as an update.
3. **Every story carries one line on why it matters to your customers**, unless you ask for headlines only, and a link to the original.
4. **Every link is checked before the issue goes out.** A story whose only link is dead is left out, and the last line of the issue says how many links were checked and how many were dead.
5. **Your copy arrives by email, from Overscan.** Nothing to press. Every issue is also kept on the `Issues` page.
6. **Audio, video and slides follow** on the issue's page once they pass their check.
