## What is in it

Visual effects supervised for Clint Eastwood, the Wachowskis and Ben Affleck: **Changeling**, which
won the VES Award for Outstanding Supporting Visual Effects, then **J. Edgar**, **Invictus**,
**Argo** and **Cloud Atlas**. **Atlantic Crossing**, supervised across vendors in three countries.
**LEGO Ninjago**, art directed across Danish and Chinese teams.

Current work under a live NDA is deliberately not in the reel.

## How to read it

A supervisor's reel is not a showreel of shots I personally comped. It is the work I was responsible
for: what got approved, what got scoped and budgeted, what got delivered on a date that could not
move, and the calls made when a sequence was not working. The interesting part of most of these
shots is the problem that is no longer visible in them.

If you are hiring for supervision or production, the fastest way to judge me is to pick a sequence
and ask what went wrong in it.
