### More valuable content than you could make alone, every day
Every issue gives your customers the news with the meaning attached: what changed, why it matters to them, and the original link, checked before the issue goes out.

Up to twice a week Overscan adds an extra on top: an audio overview first, then a slide deck or a short explainer video. The plumber listens in the van. The office manager reads on the train. You could not make this by hand. Now you do not have to.

Pick the one that leads your own email: read it, skim it, listen, watch or the slides, and your copy arrives with it on top. Overscan crafts each piece once, however many customers you have.

### Noticed by the right people
Send your issue on through the email tool you already use, or simply forward it, and your name and your issue number are in front of your customers every morning.

Week after week they see who knew first. Issue #41 is the proof you were right in issue #12.

Coming: Overscan sends straight to each customer, and each one picks the form they already use: the audio for the one in the van, the two-minute version for the skimmer, the slides for the one who runs the Monday meeting.

### The one they trust to know what's happening, and what it means
Over a month of mornings, you become the one the customer with a question asks first, because your issue told them first and told them why.

Most stories carry one line on why they matter to your customers, so the issue is never a link dump. A story they have already seen stays out until something changes, then comes back marked as an update. Overscan tries every link before the issue goes out, and the last line of every issue shows your readers how many were checked and how many were dead.

### Sounds like you, because you taught Overscan
"Shorter." "More on the new rules, less on trade shows." "Drop that site, add this one." Reply to any issue in your own words and the next one changes. You get a note back saying what Overscan understood, and replying UNDO takes it back. Overscan gets you a little more every week.

Every reply changes the next issue, and the ones after it.

### Your own read-later, in tomorrow's issue
Forward Overscan the newsletter you never open, or save a page from your phone or your browser, and Overscan puts what matters in your next issue under "You sent".

No customers needed for this part. Start as the reader, and send your issue on when you are ready.

### By hand
Read the trade press every morning and write it up yourself. Full control. It costs the hours you do not have, and it dies the first busy week.

### A newsletter platform
Great at sending and at growing a list. No idea what to say. You still find the news and write every word.

Fast, often free, and fine for keeping yourself informed. It is written for you. Nobody else reads it, nobody learns what you know, and your name is nowhere on it.

### How do we start, and how does it reach my customers?
A short call, then I point Overscan at the websites, forums, videos and newsletters you trust, and we describe your customers in your words. You see a week of real issues before anyone else does. Today each issue comes to you first, and you send it through the email tool you already use, or forward it. Email straight to your customers from your own address is coming.

### Does Overscan make a separate video for every customer?
No. Overscan crafts each piece once per issue, whether ten people read it or ten thousand. That is why each customer picking their own will not grow the cost with your list.

### Is AI writing it?
Yes. Overscan writes with AI, checks names and figures against the original story, tries every link before the issue goes out, and you change anything by replying. If you would not say it yourself, do not send it.

### Who owns what Overscan makes?
You do. Every issue, every website you chose, every lesson you taught Overscan.
