### More valuable content than you could make alone, every day
One morning of your trade's news becomes a letter, an audio overview, a short explainer video and a slide deck, all saying the same thing, all under your name, with the one-picture infographic coming. The plumber listens in the van. The office manager reads on the train. You could not make four pieces a day by hand. Now you do not have to.

Pick the one that leads your own email: read it, skim it, listen, watch or the slides, and your copy arrives with it on top. Coming: email straight to your customers, each picking their own. Overscan still crafts each piece once, however many customers you have.

Your name and your issue number on something your customers open every morning. The same expertise, in the form each person already consumes, with your name on it every time. Coming: each customer picks theirs straight from your email.

### The one they trust to know what's happening, and what it means
Every story carries one line on why it matters to your customers, not a link dump. A story they have already seen stays out until something changes, then comes back marked as an update. Every link is checked before the issue goes out, and the last line of every issue says so. Over a month of mornings, that is what makes you the one they ask.

### Sounds like you, because you taught Overscan
"Shorter." "More on the new rules, less on trade shows." "Drop that site, add this one." Reply to any issue in your own words and the next one changes. You get a note back saying what Overscan understood, and replying UNDO takes it back. No settings page, no manual: you teach Overscan the way you would teach a new hire, and Overscan gets you a little more every week.

Every reply changes the next issue, and the ones after it.

### How do we start, and how does it reach my customers?
A short call, then we point Overscan at the websites, forums, videos and newsletters you trust, and describe your customers in your words. You see a week of real issues before anyone else does. Email straight to your customers from your own address is coming; until then each issue comes to you first, and you send it through the tool you already use.

### Does Overscan make a separate video for every customer?
No. Overscan crafts each piece once per issue, whether ten people read it or ten thousand. That is why each customer picking their own will not grow the cost with your list.

### Is AI writing it?
Overscan crafts it, you decide what carries your name, and you change anything by replying. Every story links to the original. If you would not say it yourself, do not send it.

### Who owns what Overscan makes?
You do. Every issue, every website you chose, every lesson you taught Overscan.

### What does it cost?
A paid setup, quoted after one call. No tiers to decode, no self-serve plan, no public price yet.
