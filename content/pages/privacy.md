Short version: I use your details only for what you asked for. Nothing else. Never sold, never shared.

## The course waitlist

**What I collect.** Your email, your name if you give it, and any question you send. That is all.

**Why.** To email you the "Shoots You in the Foot" list and to tell you when a course date is set. The lawful basis is your consent, given when you join the waitlist.

**How long.** Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it.

## The work-with-me form

This is the form on [Work with me](/work-with-me/) and [Overscan](/overscan/).

**What I collect.** Your name, your email, your company if you give it, which offer you picked, your message, which page you sent it from, and when you ticked the box agreeing that I may contact you. To stop spam, the form also keeps a scrambled, one-way code made from your internet address, used only to count how many messages come from one place. It cannot be turned back into the address.

**Why.** To read your message and reply to it, because you asked me to. Nothing is sent to you automatically, and sending the form does not add you to any list or newsletter. Your message is not given to any AI service.

**Where.** Your message is stored in a private database on a server I run, reached through Cloudflare, and a copy is emailed to my own Google Workspace mailbox so I see it.

**How long.** The stored message is deleted automatically two years after you send it, or sooner if you ask. If we end up working together, what we agree is kept as business records for as long as the law requires.

## The VFX Supervisor Daily subscribe form

This is the form on [The VFX Supervisor Daily](/daily/) and on every issue.

**What I collect.** Your email, your first name if you give it, which format you picked (read, hear, watch, slides or one picture), which page you sent it from, and the exact words of the box you ticked, with the time you ticked it. To stop spam, the form also keeps a scrambled, one-way code made from your internet address, used only to count how many sign-ups come from one place. It cannot be turned back into the address.

**Why.** To send you one email asking you to confirm, once the Daily goes out by email, and then the Daily in the format you picked, only if you confirm. The lawful basis is your consent. Saving your spot sends you nothing today: no welcome email, no list, until that one confirmation email.

**Where.** In the same private database on a server I run, reached through Cloudflare. Before the first email goes out, this page will also name the email service that sends it.

**How long.** If you confirm, until you unsubscribe (one click in every email) or ask me to delete it. If you do not confirm, your address is deleted 7 days after that one confirmation email. A record that you agreed, and to which words, is kept for two years after you unsubscribe, because Danish law asks me to be able to show it.

## Your rights

You can ask me what I hold, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time. Every waitlist email and every issue of the Daily includes how to unsubscribe. To exercise any of this, reply to one of my emails or write to geoff@thevfxsupervisor.com, and I will delete both the stored message and the copy in my mailbox.

**Who runs this.** Geoffrey Hancock, Wangle Media ApS. Waitlist signups are stored in a private Google Sheet, work-with-me messages and Daily sign-ups in a private database on a server I run, and my own emails are sent through Google Workspace. That is the whole stack.
