The VFX Supervisor Daily · #24 · 4 November 2026

A French mocap stage seeks rescue while Weta FX bets on AI in Taiwan

Capture capacity in Europe is up for grabs as outsourcing budgets stay tight, and Weta FX is aiming at AI-led production in Asia. Worth watching for partnerships, pricing pressure and where pipeline talent gets trained.

Update: Mocap stage seeks a buyer

Update: Ex Persona is now in judicial reorganisation and open to a buyer or investor, preferably an industry partner. A fully equipped European capture stage may become available to a partner, and the cause given is weak outsourcing demand, so bidding pressure may persist.

  • Motion Capture Studio Ex Persona Seeks Buyer or Investor Amid Judicial Reorganization · 3dvf.com A Strasbourg capture stage with Vicon and Unreal-ready delivery could become a partner option for your European bids, if a deal lands

Update: Hiring includes AI-assisted VFX roles

Update: the 3 November roundup lists 28 openings, with DNEG and Dream Theory among the most active. Dream Theory is advertising a VFX producer and a VFX production manager at an AI-assisted studio, on short contracts. That offers a rate and structure reference for your own bids.

  • AWN Jobs Roundup, November 3: 28 Animation and VFX Openings Worldwide · awn.com Posted pay bands (for example Eyeline compositor at CAD 100,000-130,000) and short AI-studio contracts give you current benchmarks for rates

Notable shows and who did the work

Vendor breakdowns show who gets which kind of work. Reacher Season 4 credits BOT VFX with discreet wire removals and added gore, the sort of invisible work that is steady and worth pitching for.

  • BRINGING REALISM TO THE POST-APOCALYPTIC WORLD OF THE DOG STARS · vfxvoice.com
  • Reacher Season 4: A Closer Look at the Invisible VFX by BOT VFX · artofvfx.com Shows BOT VFX getting invisible clean-up and gore work on a returning series, a useful reference for competitor sizing

AI and production hubs

Weta FX and TAICCA plan joint investment, AI research and development, and training, with details held back until the 6 November launch. This could signal a major vendor building AI capacity outside the usual hubs. Worth watching for pricing and talent effects.

  • Weta FX and TAICCA Partner to Turn Taiwan Into an AI Screen Production Hub for Asia (EXCLUSIVE) · variety.com A vendor tying AI work to state-backed investment and training in Taiwan could shift where capacity and talent sit, so watch the 6 November details
