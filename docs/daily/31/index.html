The VFX Supervisor Daily · #31 · 11 November 2026

Owner-funded VFX, cheaper stages and AI cost claims all bear on your next bid

Virtual production is getting smaller and cheaper, and AI vendors are now putting numbers on savings, which clients may quote back at you. Big VFX work is also being steered by owners and consolidating parents. Expect pressure on price and on who holds the work.

Update: Manchester gets a 44m stage

Update: a temporary 44m by 5m volume was built at Versa in Manchester with Mars Volume, so large LED capacity can be assembled for a single job. Worth asking whether a pop-up volume suits a one-off shoot before you quote for a fixed stage.

  • Versa installs 44m long VP stage in Manchester · broadcastnow.co.uk A temporary stage of this size, built with a partner for one event, is a model you could price a short volume shoot against

Update: Nuke Stage heads to the Mac

Update: Foundry says Nuke Stage macOS support arrives in early access in 2027, with DMX lighting and output mapping added. Small in-camera VFX setups could get cheaper, which may undercut quotes for smaller volume work.

  • Foundry’s Nuke Stage macOS Support and Education Licensing · content-technology.com Mac-based in-camera VFX would lower the entry cost for small stages and may pull smaller jobs away from big volumes

Update: Ramayana is funded by its VFX owner

Update: Namit Malhotra, DNEG's CEO, says he is funding Ramayana with his own money, and DNEG is in charge of its visual effects. The film also uses AI-assisted lip-synced dubbing from Hindi to English. That suggests owner-backed projects may keep VFX in-house, leaving independents fewer bids.

  • The $500 million gamble to make India’s ‘Ramayana’ a global phenomenon · cnn.com Owner-funded projects with a VFX studio in charge may never reach an open bid, so independents should look for overflow work

AI tools and cost claims reach the pipeline

Vendors now quote cost and time savings, and clients may repeat them when you bid. The more credible tools keep artists in control: editable 3D, preserved source media. Treat Epic's agent-built preview as a test, not a production choice.

  • Animaj Unveils FlipMove AI Animation Workflow · Animation World Network
  • Avid’s Ready to Play brings AI video generation directly into Media Composer · provideocoalition.com Editors can generate temp shots and remove items like a boom mic, which may shift what clients expect to see before final VFX
  • Unreal Engine 5.9 divides game devs with an AI-heavy update · creativebloq.com/3d Epic says parts of the agent-written code are prototypes, so staying on a stable release for live jobs looks prudent
  • CAA and Ogilvy Unveil ECHO AI Celebrity Ad Platform · Animation World Network Ogilvy's new ECHO platform licenses the likeness, voice and IP of CAA clients who opt in and turns a short shoot into many assets, which could squeeze commercial shoot and comp budgets.
  • Virtual Production Finds Its Middle Ground with Blackmagic · content-technology.com Neon Vision puts about 80% of its LED work on 2D backgrounds, a cheaper volume option you could offer small clients

Cuts, new funding rules and credited work

Consolidation is cutting client-side teams, and Italy is rebuilding its incentive system. Credited work shows who wins big shows: studios that join at bidding stage and bring specialist skills.

  • Skydance Games Cuts Staff, Scales Back Projects · Animation World Network Skydance's cuts follow a $6 billion savings target, so expect tougher vendor terms from a consolidating parent
  • Italy establishes a new Cinema and Audiovisual Agency - Institutions / Legislation - Italy · cineuropa.org Italy's tax credit and co-production detail is still to come in implementing decrees, so it is worth watching if you bid for European work
  • JUSTICE PREVAILS FOR DNEG AND COYOTE VS. ACME · vfxvoice.com DNEG used a cell-shaded pipeline and mixed animators with VFX artists to match 2D cartoon characters, a skill set you can pitch
  • Project Hail Mary: Stuart Penn & Bob Winter (VFX Supervisors) and Arslan Elver (Senior Animation Supervisor) – Framestore · artofvfx.com
