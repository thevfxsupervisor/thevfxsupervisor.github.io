Italy's reform and studio ownership moves reshape where and with whom to bid

Italy has passed its film reform but left the tax credit details for later, so it is a watch item rather than a bidding lever.

Tax incentives and policy

Italy's reform promises easier permits and co-production rules, but no new credit rates yet. Those depend on decrees due within two years. Worth tracking if you pitch Italian shoots, but do not build a budget on it. Pact's note on UK employment rights is a compliance point for UK-based work.

  • Italy passes sweeping film and TV reform, with measures to attract international shoots · screendaily.com Italy's credit rates are unchanged for now, so treat it as a future option for incoming shoots and not a bid assumption
  • Pact Homepage · pact.co.uk New UK employer duties on union access and harassment are now in effect, which affects any UK company you run or hire through

Update: Weta FX and Taiwan

Update: Weta is investing in Taiwanese house Moonshine Studio and setting up a joint venture to bring local work up to international standard. It is still thin on pricing, so the question is whether Taiwan becomes a credible sub-vendor option.

  • Weta FX Partners With TAICCA To Position Taiwan As AI Hub; Invest In Moonshine Studio · uk.news.yahoo.com A named investment in Moonshine and a joint venture suggest a new Asian vendor option tied to Weta, though rates and capacity are not stated

Studio deals and ownership

Independent studios are finding ways to scale. 1920 VFX stays independently owned after a management buyout, with a Bangalore site opened in 2025. EightyEight Pictures and WildBrain have struck a CG production partnership, which could signal how animation studios share CG capacity, though the details are still worth watching. If you sub work out, expect more partners with India-based production arms.

  • ​1920 VFX completes founder-backed management buyout · shots.net A stable, investor-backed independent London vendor with a Bangalore site is a possible partner or benchmark for your own bids
  • WildBrain, EightyEight Pictures Strike CG Production Partnership · awn.com Shows a client outsourcing CG execution to an India-based vendor while keeping creative control in-house, a model you may be asked to match

Virtual production and new tools

Vancouver LED capacity is being rebuilt after Sony's Pixomondo closure, and Dark Slope is also launching a consulting arm for custom stage builds. That gives you another route to a volume in Canada. Training and tool items show where crew skills and pipelines are heading.

  • Dark Slope to Open New Virtual Production Stage in Vancouver (Exclusive) · hollywoodreporter.com A new Vancouver LED stage from December gives you a volume option in an incentive-rich market, plus a consultancy for custom builds
  • How to get more women working with virtual production in the Nordics? · nordiskfilmogtvfond.com Shows virtual production skills are being actively trained in the Nordics, which may widen the crew pool outside the usual hubs
  • fxguide · fxguide.com Headlines point to generative AI and Nuke plugin developments, worth a look for pipeline planning, though the excerpt gives no detail

Notable shows and who did the work

Vendor splits on recent shows are useful references for how work gets divided. Weta FX led Resident Evil and Moana with smaller specialists alongside, while Silverlake Studios was sole vendor on a 400-shot series.

  • The Pedestrian, Mutant Bryan and all the crazy creatures in Zach Cregger’s ‘Resident Evil’ · befores & afters Resident Evil's 708 shots were split across Weta FX, UPP, Wylie Co. and Lola, a template for a multi-vendor bid
  • fxpodcast: Wētā FX brings the ocean, lava and islands of Moana to life · fxguide Weta FX's 441 shots on Moana show how a lead vendor can take a defined chunk, such as the third act, of a larger show
  • The Drop: A Snowfall Saga – A Look at the VFX Created by Silverlake Studios · artofvfx.com A sole-vendor award of over 400 shots on a streaming series shows mid-size studios can win whole shows
