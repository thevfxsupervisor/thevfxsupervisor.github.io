What changed in VFX today, and what that means for your next bid.

Latest issue · #38 · 18 November 2026

Early involvement is where VFX work grows, as studios and clients reshape

Visualisation and early-stage involvement are turning short jobs into long ones, while client budgets shift towards cinemas and international originals. For an independent, the edge may go to whoever joins at preproduction and brings tech-led workflows. Worth watching: Equity, the UK performers' union, says it secured the first collective AI protections for performers in UK film and television, which could shape future deals.

Framestore's virtual production on Project Hail Mary

Update: the job began as a six-week visualisation assignment and became a year-long collaboration at Shepperton. That suggests a small early-stage visualisation job can grow into a much larger engagement, so it may be worth pricing it as a way in, not a standalone line.

  • How Framestore Used Virtual Production for Project Hail Mary · artofvfx.com How early virtual production work can expand.

Theatrical rebound, Netflix turmoil

Ticket sales are up 20 percent, yet cord-cutting, streaming struggles and costly mergers have Hollywood nervous, which suggests a box office rebound alone may not steady the wider business. That likely means more big-screen demand, and a shakier set of streaming contacts.

  • Movie Theaters Are Back, but the Rest of Hollywood Seems to Be Crumbling · indiewire.com Check who now holds your Netflix relationships after the purge, and weigh theatrical-grade work as demand firms up

New places and formats for VFX work

Update: DNEG is converting films to stereo 3D for Meta's VR glasses, launching spring 2027, and Ireland's 40% film tax incentive is the focus of World VFX Day on 8 December. Both could open new routes to work, though the evidence so far is thin.

  • 'Ramayana' VFX Firm DNEG Teams With Meta on 3D Movies for VR Glasses · variety.com Stereo conversion for headsets is a new delivery format, though DNEG is the named partner, so independents may only see subcontract work
  • World VFX Day to broadcast live from Dublin on 8th December · scannain.com A free livestream from Dublin on 8 December is a cheap way to hear studios' views on the Irish incentive before you bid

Credited work: who did what, and how

Three shows show where vendor work is going. Avatar moved back to locations and bluescreen after leaning on the volume in Season 1. Tenzing brought VFX in during early preproduction. Proof is building a presence in New York. Early-stage specialists look likely to be your competition and your partners.

  • Bending in a Believable Way: 'Avatar: The Last Airbender' Season 2 VFX · Animation World Network A franchise stepping back from the volume towards location shoots and bluescreen extensions affects what a bid should assume
  • Go behind the scenes of ‘Tenzing’ with this VFX reel · befores & afters Five named vendors shared 630 shots, and the supervisor says VFX was a production partner from early preproduction
  • Proof Shares 'Coyote vs. Acme' Postvis Reel · awn.com Proof's seven-week postvis job and new New York office show visualisation shops expanding, as rivals or partners for pitch-stage work

Ownership, budgets and labour

UK stage operators are under cost pressure and leaning on virtual production. Disney is raising content spend but has not said how much goes to international originals. Equity's AI terms may set a benchmark for scanning and digital likeness.

  • Arts Alliance acquires Garden Studios · televisual.com Garden Studios now has a single owner and plans to focus on tech-led workflows while reviewing its physical footprint, so check any stage booking terms
  • Disney raises 2027 content budget 9% to US$26bn, targets more local originals · More commissioning opportunities for local producers. A 9% rise to US$26bn and tripled international originals could mean more regional VFX work, but the split is not stated
  • Union Equity secures UK-first AI protections for performers · c21media.net
