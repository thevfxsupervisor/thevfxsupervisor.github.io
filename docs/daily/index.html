Camera-mounted capture and supplier upheaval could change how you bid

On-set depth capture is moving closer to the camera, which may change what you quote for matchmove. Meanwhile ownership and leadership shifts at studios, suppliers and a UK stage site are worth tracking before you commit to partners or locations.

Update: Mantis puts depth capture on the camera

Update: Hexagon has now launched Mantis, a solid-state LiDAR that records depth frame by frame as the camera rolls. If it works as described, matchmove and set-scan assumptions in your bids may shift. Pricing and real-world results are not given, so treat it as worth watching.

  • Hexagon Mounts a LiDAR on the Cinema Camera Itself to Kill VFX’s Costliest Bottleneck · techtimes.com Claims a per-frame spatial record and Alembic output feeding Unreal, which could trim matchmove cost and LED-stage calibration work if it holds up
  • Hexagon’s New Mantis On-Set 3D Capture System for VFX · postperspective.com Confirms the launch and the standard EXR, FBX and Alembic outputs, so it should slot into existing pipelines without a rebuild

Update: Gedeon Media's assets land with Autentic

Update: Autentic has been confirmed as the winning bidder, and production continues through a new Paris arm, Gedeon Studios, led by Carine Nonnenmacher. This is factual TV, so the VFX impact is small. It suggests distressed European producers are being absorbed rather than closed.

  • Autentic acquires Gedeon Media’s assets and sets up new studio arm · c21media.net A sign that buyers are consolidating failed European producers; if you pitch to such outfits, expect new owners and smaller teams

Studio ownership and space

Ownership changes and facility risk affect who you can partner with and where you can shoot or book space. Check the current owner before you subcontract or tie a bid to a facility.

  • Could Elstree Film Studios be put up for sale? · tvbeurope.com The council says continued investment is not sustainable and alternative uses are on the table, so UK stage availability at Elstree is worth watching
  • CCH hands VFX studio Wanget back to its founder via MBO · app.dealroom.co

Supplier and platform risk

Your pipeline leans on a few big technology suppliers, and both are in flux. Neither changes your bid today, but they are worth watching for pricing, roadmap and AI-workflow shifts.

  • Jensen Huang Out as NVIDIA CEO · Animation World Network
  • Vizrt acquires digital broadcast titling and graphics systems company Chyron · tvbeurope.com Suggests broadcast tooling is consolidating around AI platforms, which may bring new workflow demands if you do live or graphics work

Who did what on current shows

These credits show how vendors are split on current work and which practical-versus-digital choices clients accept. Useful reference when you pitch similar creature or episodic work.

  • Female leadership in VFX for action-crime series Neagley – behind the scenes with WeFX · britishcinematographer.co.uk A Toronto vendor delivered more than 250 shots with about 70 artists in eight months, a useful benchmark for mid-size episodic scope
  • Below: Matt Glover (Production VFX Supervisor) and Annie Godin (Production VFX Producer) · artofvfx.com Shows a lead vendor taking about two thirds of a show, with smaller shops brought in for CG fire and overflow when the schedule tightened
  • How Ludo Studio Made 'Allen': Gecko Smiles, Eight Puppets and an Animatronic That "Didn't Fly" · whats-on-netflix.com A full animatronic failed a test with Netflix and the team switched to puppets, a reminder that practical creature routes can still win
  • Discover how EDI created a photorealistic CG jaguar for Ferine · cgchannel.com A small Italian house built a photorealistic CG creature, useful as a reference for expressive animal work on a modest budget
