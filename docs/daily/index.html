What changed in VFX today, and what that means for your next bid.

Latest issue · #41 · 21 November 2026

Utopai plans 500 jobs while a US film credit gathers support

AI-led production is moving from debate to hiring plans and tooling that overlaps with VFX work. Meanwhile, the US credit picked up cosponsors but still has no vote date. Watch who gets the AI-assisted budgets, and keep any US credit out of bids until it passes.

What this means for you

AI is arriving inside tools you already use: Avid's Ready to Play in Media Composer, Adobe agents in After Effects and Premiere, and an AI-heavy Unreal Engine 5.9, so expect clients to ask about it in bids. [1, 2, 3, 4] Avid's Ready to Play is free to Ultimate and Enterprise users, but Google's models need an eligible Google Cloud account, so price that dependency into any editorial or post offer. [2] Framestore combined traditional previs with AI tools on Sky's Christmas campaign, a sign that hybrid methods may be pitched as standard, so decide where you will use AI and say so. [5] Equity's UK collective AI protections for performers and the Disney and Adobe alliance on custom models suggest rules and client-owned models are taking shape, so check contracts for consent and data use. [6, 7]

AI in production

Tools that relight, add extras, swap green screen and generate new angles from live footage overlap with work you may bid on. Clients may start asking what can be done with AI instead. Be ready to say what you would use and what you would not.

  • David Fincher Defends Use Of AI Amid ‘Cliff Booth’ Criticism: “Well, It Exists. Should We Not Use It?” · deadline.com Fincher defending AI use on Cliff Booth after criticism suggests clients may keep asking you where AI fits in your pipeline
  • World’s first hybrid film production platform debuts · tvbeurope.com Relight, extras, green-screen replacement and new angles from existing footage overlap with shot work you might otherwise bid, so expect questions on price

AI-native studios hiring

Utopai is one AI-native studio, so treat this as a possible sign, not a trend. Its claim that about 80% of budget goes to people is worth testing. If it hires out as well as in-house, it could become a client or a competitor.

  • Utopai Studios Opens Beverly Hills Office · Animation World Network A studio planning about 500 production jobs worldwide in 2027 may become a buyer of senior VFX talent, or a rival for the same budgets

Leadership moves

Fremantle is losing the executive who set up its AI-focused label, Imaginae Studios. Whether that label keeps its priority is worth watching if you pitch AI-adjacent work to the group. Scrosati's new venture could also become a client.

  • Fremantle COO and CEO for Europe Andrea Scrosati set to step down · c21media.net Scrosati leaves at the end of January to start his own venture, which may change who champions Imaginae's AI work at Fremantle

Notable shows and who did the work

Both items are useful credit references for pitching. One is invisible work on a comedy, the other a vendor recreating a creator-built environment. They show the kinds of shots that win clients.

  • All the invisible effects in ‘Ted Lasso’, season 4 · befores & afters VFX supervisor James MacLachlan on Ted Lasso season 4: football play, crowds and stadiums sit alongside hidden fixes, a useful reference for invisible-work bids
  • Backrooms: CoSA VFX Reveals the Visual Effects Behind the Threshold Chamber · artofvfx.com CoSA VFX rebuilt the Backrooms Threshold Chamber from the creator's own Blender file, a case study in blending a client's asset with live action
