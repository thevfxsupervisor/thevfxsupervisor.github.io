Hybrid AI pipelines reach paid VFX work as DC and Netflix buyers shift

Framestore's Sky spot puts AI inside a conventional VFX pipeline on a paid job, while cinematographers argue about it publicly. Meanwhile DC gets a new creative chief and buyers keep consolidating. Worth watching: how clients start asking for AI in bids, and who now signs off at DC.

AI and volumes inside real pipelines

Framestore used AI for previs, environments and character removal, with named supervisors and human control kept in place. That is a template for how clients may expect AI to be priced and credited. The volume piece is a reminder to scope LED work honestly: wide shots can end up as full VFX.

  • Framestore Brings Hybrid AI/VFX to Sky's Christmas Campaign · awn.com Gives you the credit structure and task split of a hybrid AI/VFX job, useful when writing your own AI line items
  • Framestore delivers previs, hybrid AI and animation for Sky’s Christmas campaign · tvbeurope.com Shows AI used for previs, matte painting and character removal alongside traditional comp, a model for pitching controlled AI use
  • Cinematographers’ Favorite Festival Is All About AI This Year, but Artists Still Hate the Tech · indiewire.com Cinematographers voiced bleak views while directors sounded more open, so expect clients on the same job to disagree about AI
  • Standardising the illusion · britishcinematographer.co.uk A working DP says true wide shots in a volume usually become full VFX shots, which affects how you scope and bid
  • Cloud Imperium Games expands VERSA Studios partnership with Squadron 42 showcase and BTS filming · britishcinematographer.co.uk A new large volume in Manchester, built with a specialist partner, is another UK stage option, though this job was event work

DC leadership change

Gunn and Safran leave DC's top jobs and Jeremy Latcham takes over on 1 December, reporting into Skydance. A new head of film, TV and animation could reshape slate priorities and vendor relationships, which may open or close doors. Treat that as interpretation until his first decisions land.

  • DC Studios Shakeup: James Gunn, Peter Safran Stepping Down as Co-CEOs, Marvel Vet Jeremy Latcham Named Chief Creative Officer · indiewire.com Mixed box office and a new creative chief suggest DC's slate and vendor choices could change, so watch early announcements
  • DC Studios Co-Chiefs James Gunn, Peter Safran Step Down · Animation World Network

Update: incentives and where productions go

Update: Ireland is now the clearest example of runaway production chasing incentives, with record spend and stages often full. Canada has also now said how its C$600m funding package will be split. Both affect where your shows shoot and which co-production routes are open.

  • Carney gov’t sets out how Canada’s $425m funding boost will be shared · Canada's federal government has set out how its C$600m (US$425m) relief package for audiovisual and audio industries will be shared, which could ease funding pressure for producers. Money flows mainly through existing Canadian funders, with the CMF running new programmes, so check what reaches production and training

Update: who delivered the work

Update: the Mandalorian issue names the vendor split behind ILM, and the Godzilla piece shows a VFX-led team reusing ride research. Cliff Booth shows a smaller named vendor on a Fincher feature. Useful for sizing competitors and for pitch references.

  • Mando’s miniatures, puppets, stop-motion and more · befores & afters Shows ILM-led work farmed to Image Engine, Hybride, Lola and others, plus Tippett on stop-motion: a map of who gets overflow
  • King Ghidorah Is Ready for Its Close-Up: How ‘Godzilla Minus Zero’ Cracked the Legendary Monster’s Next Evolution · indiewire.com Creature design reused from a theme-park ride shows prior R&D can become a selling point on later films
  • The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth · artofvfx.com Ollin VFX is credited on a Fincher feature releasing this month, worth tracking as a rival on prestige work
