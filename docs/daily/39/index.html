Skydance consolidation and AI tooling both change who you bid to and how

Skydance is merging studios and chasing savings, which may mean fewer buyers and tighter budgets for outside vendors. Autodesk is putting agents inside Maya and 3ds Max, while clients already use AI without always saying so. Worth writing AI terms into your bids now.

Skydance consolidation narrows the buyer list

Studios are merging and a streamer is closing, so there may be fewer commissioning doors and more cost pressure on vendors. With SkyShowtime set to shut down and its CEO exiting, it is worth watching who ends up approving VFX budgets for the projects it leaves behind.

  • SkyShowtime to Shut Down, CEO Exiting · hollywoodreporter.com SkyShowtime, the joint venture of Comcast and then-named ViacomCBS, is shutting down, which could shrink buyer options for programming; worth watching how Comcast responds.
  • Warner Bros TV Group, Paramount TV Studios set to merge in latest Skydance re-org · c21media.net
  • David Ellison on Elon Musk’s Skydance Investment, Trump and ‘Rush Hour 4,’ and Whether He Would’ve Made ‘Digger’ · indiewire.com Ellison says budgets will be sized to audience, a hint that bids may be tested against projected reach

AI moves into the pipeline, rules lag behind

Agents inside Maya and 3ds Max are in tech preview, and one artist reports big time savings on tool testing. Meanwhile many creatives use AI on client work without telling the client. Spell out AI use and disclosure in your contracts and bids.

  • Autodesk Adds Agentic AI and MCP Servers to Maya and 3ds Max · Animation World Network, also reported by postperspective.com Read-write agent access is off by default; one artist cut tool testing from over an hour to about five minutes, so test before promising savings
  • Envato's 2nd Annual AI Report Finds Adoption Has Outrun the Rules · Animation World Network With 53% of AI users skipping disclosure and 57% of teams lacking a written policy, clear contract terms could set you apart

Shows, credits and how work gets won

The Odyssey panel argues for bringing VFX in during prep, a useful line when you pitch for early involvement and budget. The other items show how work is split and won: eight named vendors on one series, and a regional studio that self-funded its way to features.

  • Christopher Nolan and Guillermo del Toro Break Down ‘The Odyssey’ Monsters and AI’s Limitations with Production VFX Supervisor Andrew Jackson · indiewire.com Nolan and del Toro back early VFX integration, which supports pitching for prep-stage involvement rather than late rescue work
  • 3 Body Problem – Season 2 · artofvfx.com
  • Post uncovered: Flipbook Studio delivers invisible VFX for Dennis Quaid thriller · broadcastnow.co.uk Flipbook says self-funded fan film shots led to its first feature, one possible route for a studio outside London

New hire at SSVFX, first title in DNEG's Meta work

Update: SSVFX has named Jennifer Thompson as Head of Studio, with a brief to pursue feature and high-end TV work and international partners. DNEG has named The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping as its first Meta conversion, arriving after the theatrical run. Both are worth watching as possible routes to work.

  • ‘Shōgun’ Post House SSVFX Hires Jennifer Thompson To Run Studio Operations · deadline.com SSVFX is pushing for more outside work
  • DNEG Converting Film Catalog to 3D for Meta VR Glasses · Animation World Network A named first title, released to the glasses after theatrical, shows the conversion work is real; the catalogue scale is still unclear
