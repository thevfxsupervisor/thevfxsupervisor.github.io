AI is moving into Nuke and Resolve, so bid for new workflows

Both main finishing tools now plug into AI assistants, which may shift how you scope compositing and post hours. Meanwhile UK studio money keeps flowing, and cross-border vendor ties are winning real shot counts. Worth checking pipeline assumptions before the next bid.

Studios and incentives

UK public money is backing new stage space outside London. More capacity could ease competition for stages and may widen where UK-based productions shoot, which matters if you bid against or alongside British vendors. The NSI lab headline hints at similar training investment elsewhere, but there is no detail to act on.

  • UK Budget promises £20 million for ‘Hollywood of the North’ · tvbeurope.com Two new sound stages in Liverpool, backed by £20 million, may add UK capacity and regional competition for shows you pitch for
  • NSI launches AI, virtual production lab in Manitoba · playbackonline.ca A Canadian AI and virtual production lab is a sign of public money going into these skills, though the headline alone gives no detail

AI in pipelines

Nuke and Resolve are both gaining AI hooks. Generative elements inside the comp graph and assistant-driven automation in post could trim routine hours, so your bids may need to say where AI is allowed and how it is priced. Nuke 18.0 is only in beta, so treat it as something to test.

  • Foundry releases Nuke 18.0 in open beta · cgchannel.com A Griptape node in Nuke lets compositors generate elements in the node graph, which may change how you scope cleanup and element work
  • How AI assistants are speeding up post production · tvbeurope.com Resolve's assistant support automates metadata, versioning and batch tasks, which suggests lower post hours for repetitive work, worth testing before pricing

Notable shows and credits

Invisible work like environment extensions and small digital fixes is a steady source of work on series. Seeing how a vendor presents it shows what producers value in a showreel for this kind of job.

  • Lanterns: Discover CoSA VFX’s Invisible Effects, From Digital Landscapes to a Beard · artofvfx.com CoSA VFX's Lanterns work shows subtle environment extension is credited work worth pitching, and who to watch for similar series bids

Vendor partnerships

An Indian studio won 300 shots on a feature through a regional producer relationship built on trust. It suggests overflow work can go to well-connected partners, which is worth knowing when you weigh outsourcing or compete with it.

  • Beyond the Render: Craft, Hospitality and the Human side of Cross-Border VFX, LBB · lbbonline.com Resonance landing 300 shots on Land of Bad via a regional bridge shows the kind of offshore competition you may face on bids
